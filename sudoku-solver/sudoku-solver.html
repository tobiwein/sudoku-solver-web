Sudoku Solver

  • Enter the known numbers in the Sudoku grid. Only numbers from 1 to 9 are accepted.
  • Once you have entered everything, click on ‘Check‘. Any invalid digits will now be marked with a red frame. If everything is correct, the numbers you have entered will be marked with a gray frame.
  • The ‘Solve‘ button will be activated if everything is correct. Press this button to solve the Sudoku puzzle.
  • The ‘Clear‘ button can be pressed at any time to clear the entire grid.
